## AI Disruption
- AI can process large amounts of data quickly
- Reduces need for manual data analysis
- Automates tasks like image recognition and signal analysis
- Some repetitive tasks may be replaced by AI

---

## AI Disruption (Continued)
- AI can generate reports and summaries
- Reduces time spent writing intelligence reports
- Role may shift from creating to reviewing information
- Requires adaptation to new technology

---

## AI Benefits
- Faster and more efficient data analysis
- Helps identify patterns and potential threats
- Improves accuracy of intelligence work
- Allows officers to focus on decision-making

## AI Benefits (Continued)

- Organizes complex information clearly
- Provides real-time insights
- Enhances overall performance
